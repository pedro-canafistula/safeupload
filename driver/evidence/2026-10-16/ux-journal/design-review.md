# T4/T11 unbound terminal journal scaling — independent design review

2026-10-16. Requested reviewer: Sol 6.1, xhigh. Source inspected: `2a2a2b8973bcb30dadbea7a0ec4b669448bbed78` (`ux/journal`), the initial `design.md`, current owner decisions, transfer journal/compactor, unbound namespace journal, publisher/recovery, startup/worker dispatch and journal file/replacement helpers. Review only; no source changes, build, VM run or activation.

**Verdict: REVISE the recovery/integrity details before implementing archive authority.** The bounded segments, exact immutable manifests, hot-only dispatch, selected-claim reads and preservation of fresh destination-head validation are a sound direction. Retaining exact receipts and generation ties is essential. This is a design verdict, not implementation acceptance or protected-pair qualification.

## Required revisions

1. **An incomplete tail is not by itself proof of an unfinished append.** Once a flushed frame's hot manifest has been deleted, later truncation or damage to its length field can make the sole durable generation/rename/discard receipt look like an incomplete tail. Silently dropping it can rebuild an empty/lower head and reuse a generation. Define hash coverage for the framing fields, version/magic, checked lengths/offsets and the positive evidence that permits truncation. Fail closed on an ambiguous tail; alternatively use a durable append-intent/frontier with independently validated surviving hot evidence, or atomically replace a bounded segment. Merely adding a checksum inside the disappearing frame does not authenticate its absence. After any uncertain append/flush/truncate, prohibit another append until recovery establishes a valid committed boundary; never append after an unexamined partial frame.

2. **Specify the archive index's authority and failure lifecycle.** Startup must finish full archive validation before the journal answers allocation, replay, generation or absence questions. A runtime append/rewrite error, missing segment, checksum/schema mismatch, conflicting duplicate or stale indexed location must invalidate/latch the affected authority and force complete recovery before further authoritative use. Selected reads must fresh-validate the actual opened segment and verify the expected GUID, destination claim/generation/tombstone and frame binding, including every maximum tie. Rebuild/publish offsets and claims under the same journal gate after a rewrite; handle a rename that committed before an exception without retaining old offsets. Compaction currently catches/logs failures in `MinifilterInterceptor.RecoverStagingSubsystem`; archive integrity failure cannot fall through that path and leave staging usable with a partial index.

   The draft deliberately delays discovery of unrelated archived corruption. State that scoped integrity contract explicitly, including validation after an I/O error/hold and an idle sweep, or obtain acceptance of the narrower schedule. The 2026-10-15 plan approval concerns the separate semantic/V30 store and includes after-error and idle validation; it is not blanket approval to weaken every journal's checks. Keep bound records/heads on their existing full-validation path. Absence answers must come only from a completely validated startup snapshot plus successfully committed local updates, not a partially rebuilt or held index.

3. **Make live-epoch and obligation retention executable, independent of age.** Keep the full manifests/rename/discard/cancellation receipts for the accepted live driver epoch even when timestamps precede the cutoff; a wall-clock step must not make current replay facts collectible. Preserve reference closure over the union of hot and archived records, maximum ties, all pending head/receipt owners and allocation-security references before any segment replacement/deletion. Define treatment of legacy records without an epoch proof. The existing preboot-minus-one-day cutoff alone is not evidence that a known live epoch ended. Include cancellation receipts explicitly: `StagedUnboundCancellation.Generation` exceeds the manifest's `DestinationGeneration`, whereas current destination/compaction projections only enumerate the latter and namespace tombstones. Prove the intended cancellation floor/replay semantics survive archival and retention; do not accidentally treat that receipt as ordinary historical `Retained` data.

## Implementation conditions

- Archive only an exact fresh version after the caller's audit/notification/justification/disposal obligations finish. The completion signal must identify the version, and archive admission must recheck it under the journal gate. A failed or ambiguous callback stays hot. Startup recovery must establish the same condition rather than equating `Released` or `Verified` with completed caller work. Keep driver-held, delete-pending and still-present stages hot, and poll released-stage last close without repeating publication.
- Admission is not just `Directory.CreateDirectory`. The current Windows constructor opens **every top-level entry** as a regular bounded file, so the new `completed` directory needs explicit trusted-directory handling. Preserve protected ancestors, no reparse traversal, trusted owner/private DACL, handle-based regular-file/single-link checks and 4 MiB segment/128 KiB payload bounds before reading or repairing anything. Do not follow or ACL-repair an unexpected child. Keep snapshots and unexpected files fail-closed.
- Enforce immutable archived IDs at the write boundary. Exact duplicate hot/archive/segment copies may be consolidated once only after complete equality; disagreement is corruption. Existing replay APIs must return their original result or refuse a changed request, never recreate a mutable hot manifest. In particular cancellation-cleanup replay currently rewrites its timestamp; make archived completion a true no-op. Frame hashes check integrity; they do not replace exact object/version/policy authority.
- `Flush(true)` before private metadata rename supports removal of redundant temporary-file `WriteThrough`; preserve the finite existing rename-only retry and public commit behavior. Do not delete a hot manifest until the archive frame is durably usable, or retire an old segment until the replacement is flushed/committed. Cancellation after a successful commit must finish/recover bookkeeping, not replay the mutation.

## Focused controls

1. Interrupt/fault before and after frame/header/payload write, flush, hot deletion, segment rollover, compaction flush/rename and index publication; restart each image. Distinguish legitimate partial append from truncation/length damage of the last **already archived** sole maximum/tombstone. Include a second append after the first fails.
2. Corrupt length, GUID, digest, JSON/schema and size; remove/replace a selected segment; introduce exact and conflicting hot/archive or archive/archive duplicates. Test index hold, full recovery, archive-directory reparse/hard-link/ACL/owner refusal and clean service restart on Windows user and SYSTEM legs.
3. Mixed hot/archive ancestry, cross-segment chains, maximum ties, rename aliases/tombstones, cancellation's separate generation, pending head/receipt owners and current-epoch records with an old timestamp. Assert exact replay and strictly preserved intended generation barriers before/after restart and interrupted compaction.
4. Inject failure after caller bookkeeping reaches disk but before/after its completion signal. Keep unfinished notifications/receipts hot; close a driver-held released stage later and prove eventual archival with one public mutation. Changed replay requests must fail without creating hot files.
5. Measure allocate + seal and worker/discard scans with 10k **real retained records**, both many destinations and repeated saves/rename claims, plus a fixed active set. Report elapsed time, allocations, actual manifest/frame reads, hot file count and retained archive bytes. Fixture setup is outside timing; startup/migration/compaction costs are reported separately. Linux feedback does not replace the required Windows four-leg matrix or protected T4 evidence.

No V30 import/activation, bound-head shortcut, cap change, threshold change or VM action is accepted by this review. One coherent implementation review can verify these conditions; no per-edit review framework is needed.
