# T9 large-file admission: independent design review

2026-10-16. Read-only product-security review of `driver/UX-LARGE.md`, current owner/status decisions, and the allocator, stage I/O, snapshot, inspection-worker, publisher, cleanup and journal source. No builds, product VM actions or Git mutations. Implementation was in progress and is not approved by this review.

**Verdict: ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS for the disabled-by-default, unbound-only local storage prototype, incorporating the primary's corrective design delta below.** The original draft alone requires revision. This is neither T9 completion nor approval to activate/cut over or boot an unreviewed kernel change.

## Required corrective delta

1. **A missing grant cannot increase authorized capacity.** The draft's absent `.space` → 16 MiB fallback otherwise exceeds any smaller reservation. The primary selected a minimum admitted content capacity of 16 MiB, with a corresponding full conservative debit. Smaller available shares refuse. Keep missing-companion fallback at that legacy limit; malformed, wrong-identity, wrong-transfer, negative/overflowing or otherwise untrusted companions refuse. Bind the immutable grant to the exact already-open stage and typed allocation identity; reopening cannot increase it.

2. **Metadata remains charged after content cleanup.** The proposed 64 KiB is insufficient: `StagedJournalFile.MaximumManifestBytes` is 128 KiB, with replacement overlap, and journals outlive the stage. The primary selected a retained 1 MiB metadata debit per transfer and explicitly excludes bound allocations/security snapshots. Document the unbound metadata upper-bound arithmetic, including reservation/grant files, manifests, replacement overlap and retained receipts. Refund content only after exact stage cleanup, no snapshot leases and a terminal state without unresolved hand-back/publication work. Retain metadata until its own durable cleanup; if no such cleanup is implemented, retain that debit indefinitely. A failed temporary deletion or uncertain mutation must remain charged and prevent a retry from adding another copy.

3. **Reconstruct all outstanding storage before opt-in admission.** Loading only new reservation records misses legacy stages, snapshots or interrupted outputs. The primary selected refusal of opt-in startup when existing objects cannot be reconciled with durable reserved identities. Persist the reservation before seed/output growth; persist each object's identity before copying into it. A crash between creation and identity recording retains the full reservation and an actionable hold. Do not delete unknown files by extension/name or refund because a manifest is missing. Missing/corrupt reservation ownership cannot create a fresh empty accounting state.

4. **Pending defaults stay proposals.** The primary selected explicit positive total/per-user byte settings and an explicit nonnegative floor for enabling the mode. `Enabled=true` alone must not silently activate the pending proposed defaults. Ordinary disabled configuration preserves the legacy profile and all existing readiness/capability gates.

## Conditions on implementation and qualification

- Acquire the reservation's single-snapshot slot **before content copying**. Persist the exact snapshot identity and keep its physical-storage debit until every duplicated reader handle has closed and exact cleanup is confirmed. Publisher timeout/disposal is not parser termination. A second capture must not grow while an earlier snapshot still exists. Abrupt service/machine interruption requires separate controls.
- Use checked signed 64-bit arithmetic throughout content limits, four-copy charges, sums and alignment. Validate already seeded EOF/allocation, append/current-pointer writes, EOF changes, allocation changes and mapping-driven growth against the immutable capacity. Include exact-limit, one-byte-over, negative and near-overflow controls. Physical allocation/sector rounding must fit the conservative debit.
- Integrity is required before ZIP approval, including members whose decoder/consumer stops early: verify the complete declared output length, CRC, final DEFLATE marker and compressed extent, and reject extra/truncated data. Every member must be fully inspected or explicitly refused; a successful selected pass or partial findings cannot approve the archive. Buffered nested seek inputs require a separate enforced bound.
- Preserve current policy/whole-object/destination-generation checks and the confirmed-native-rename commit point. Disk full or cleanup failure retains the private source and the old destination when uncommitted; a confirmed public mutation permits bookkeeping retry only. Hand-back still requires the existing caller read/export authority.
- **Parser resource isolation remains open.** `InspectionService` has two shared in-process worker slots; cancellation-ignoring parsers can retain both and their snapshots indefinitely. Per-SID disk debits and fixed scanner buffers do not bound Office/PDF allocations, isolate parser crashes or guarantee other users progress. The prototype must state this limit. Full T9 closure still needs a reviewed bounded-parser/fair-admission design, ignored-cancellation/crash controls and the required protected-pair rows.

The capacity-grant architecture is reasonable under these conditions: reserving four overlapping copies before exposure, granting no publication authority, following exact snapshot handles, and holding uncertain cleanup are compatible with the product's fail-closed invariants. The conditions above still require implementation evidence and the applicable component, kernel and matched-pair qualification gates.
