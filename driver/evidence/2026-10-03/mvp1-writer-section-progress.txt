Source changes
- bf43d8b4: fix the Verifier 0xD1 paged-scan access under fence spin locks.
- de9743cc: replace lock-free section pairing with one locked table containing
  writable and non-writable acquisitions. A release removes only the newest

  did not include it in the verdict.
