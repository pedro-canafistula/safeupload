Recommendation
==============

Keep the two checked old-and-candidate union refreshes in the uncommitted
Policy.c change as fail-closed control-plane hardening. Candidate policy is
published only after both scans succeed; a scan failure is propagated and
does not publish the candidate. This does not establish complete coverage or
close the mapped-view race. The change still needs four normal/feature
Debug/Release WDK/PREfast builds and the required agent suite. Run 22 predates
this source revision.

No additional source-only change in the current minifilter safely makes the
approved removable, SMB/UNC, and sync-client scopes ready. Keep the complete
policy flags and keep unsupported candidate coverage rejected. Removing the
rejection would turn known missing coverage into apparent policy success.

Source constraints
==================

* FenceClassifyVolume scans fixed local NTFS only; it skips removable and
  network volumes. FenceRefreshInternal rejects a candidate when those flags
  are set, and the late-attach path skips non-fixed-NTFS volumes instead of
  synchronously completing a fence.
* StageCreate rejects network and non-NTFS filesystems. StageOpenBacking
  hardcodes C: as the backing volume, requires NTFS, and checks that its device
  stack is at least as deep as the source stack. Removing those guards without
  per-stack filesystem and identity handling would not prove names, file IDs,
  aliases, rename, paging I/O, or backing I/O work on those stacks.
* The section-create callback can reject some new writable sections. It cannot
  prevent a later view from being mapped from an already-retained writable
  section handle. InstanceSetup queues a late scan instead of waiting, so it
  does not provide an admission barrier.

Required invariant and counterexamples
=======================================

Never report a candidate ready unless every applicable destination class has
an admission barrier before ordinary handles or writable sections can exist,
and admitted writes use owned-stream handling. If admission or coverage is
uncertain, remain fail-closed.

Counterexamples include a USB file with a writable section retained without a
view across policy expansion; an SMB/UNC handle or mapping opened before
attachment and written after it; and a sync-client path with a pre-existing
view or retained section. Current scans can skip or miss these, and a later
view from a retained section can escape even after a successful scan.

The repository's admission-gate design assessment already describes the
missing external or earlier per-stack gate. A source shim duplicating that
assessment would add no enforceable behavior.

Qualification gates
===================

The immediate VM gate for the current transaction change is to use the
unchanged approved policy and confirm fail-closed ERROR_NOT_SUPPORTED, with
policy generation unchanged and prior fence state preserved. This validates
rejection only. Full-scope claims require separate disposable target-stack
tests with unchanged policy: USB supported filesystems plus direct-volume and
late-attach races; SMB/UNC with two clients, server-side independent byte
observation, reconnect and retained-section cases; and the actual sync client
with independent observation of synchronized bytes. Each class also needs
pre-existing handles/views, first view after expansion, aliases and mutation
paths, failure/recovery, and full restoration before it can be claimed.
