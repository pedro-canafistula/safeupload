Scope
-----
Read-only review of the current policy-transition and stage-fence source after
the two checked pre-publication union scans replaced the unchecked post-swap
refresh. Also checked whether this source change alters the prior full-scope
conclusion for removable, SMB/UNC, or sync-client destinations.

Findings
--------
1. P1: The section/view lifetime race remains. `SafeUploadSetPolicy` keeps the
   old policy current, exposes the candidate, performs two checked union scans,
   then swaps policy (`Policy.c:403-445`). Writable-section admission and scans
   do not synchronize a retained section handle through a later view creation.
   A view can therefore be created after the scans and policy commit.
2. P1: No safe C-only patch makes removable, SMB/UNC, or sync-client scopes
   ready. `FenceClassifyVolume` covers fixed NTFS only (`StageFence.c:223-240`);
   transitions reject skipped volume scopes and reparses
   (`StageFence.c:1143-1160`). Late attachment remains asynchronous and leaves
   an attachment-to-scan window (`StageFence.c:1805-1822`, `Filter.c:531-544`).
   Staging also rejects network/non-NTFS targets and uses a C: NTFS backing
   volume (`StageStream.c:650-696,883-886`).

Disposition and next gate
-------------------------
The checked scans improve failure handling: scan failures reject the candidate
and preserve the old policy. They do not establish complete coverage or close
the section/view race. Keep unsupported destination scopes rejected. Do not add
scope-enabling code until a per-stack admission design exists.

Next validation gate: build and test the exact current source with the WDK,
then run the rejection-only policy case on a clean VM. The rejection-only case
must preserve the unchanged approved policy and prior fence state. Full USB,
SMB/UNC, and sync-client qualification remains separate and requires per-stack
admission design and independent destination-byte observation.

No WDK build, PowerShell parse, VM execution, or destination-byte observation
was performed by this review.
