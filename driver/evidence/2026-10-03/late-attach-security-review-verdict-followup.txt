Remaining P1 architecture blockers
----------------------------------

- StageStream.c:2484-2486 still completes policy-denied writable-section
  creation with STATUS_ACCESS_DENIED. The Microsoft callback contract
  documents STATUS_INSUFFICIENT_RESOURCES for failed SyncTypeCreateSection;
  this is not a supported policy-denial status:
  https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ifs/flt-parameters-for-irp-mj-acquire-for-section-synchronization
- A section callback admitted immediately before pending-policy publication
  can return SUCCESS_NO_CALLBACK and finish section creation after the
  transition's post-swap scan. See Policy.c:408-410, 447-453 and
  StageStream.c:2476-2480. Current-plus-pending matching classifies snapshots;
  it does not reserve and drain section creation.
- An unowned selected reparse FSCTL admitted before pending publication can
  remain in the lower filesystem while the scan passes, then complete before
  commit. StageStream.c:2401-2417 has no rundown through completion;
  STAGED-WRITES.md:3279-3287 accurately records this limitation.
- StageAdmit and legacy create/write/rename/publication operations still use
  current policy while candidate scopes are scanned. Operations to a newly
  added prefix can race the scan; STAGED-WRITES.md:3288-3296 records the gap.
- Late attachment still queues asynchronous scan work. A preexisting writable
  mapping can page data before its fence is installed; the InstanceSetup
  callback cannot wait under its documented contract. STAGED-WRITES.md:
  3308-3317 records the admission requirement or staging-off restriction.
- Failed or over-cap scans discard the partial table. Quarantine blocks
  protected-name opens but cannot identify omitted SOPs for read or paging
  write gates; extant mapped views also read resident pages without a filter
  callback. See StageFence.c:1172-1190 and STAGED-WRITES.md:3323-3332.
- Manual QueryTeardown is refused in the feature build, but mandatory filter
  unload or volume dismount can still remove protection. That lifecycle must
  remain outside the enabled staging contract or be covered by an external
  admission mechanism.
