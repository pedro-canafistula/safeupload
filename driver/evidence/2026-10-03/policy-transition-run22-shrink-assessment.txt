Policy-transition run22 shrink diagnostic
Date: 2026-10-03

Disposition
-----------
The reviewed harness ran on the isolated Windows 10 guest under a clean
original-driver/policy/Verifier baseline and a disk-only checkpoint. It
created its two writable mappings and captured the raw fixture baseline, but
stopped before accepting the baseline policy. The real agent timed out waiting
for policy acceptance; its log reports `FilterSendMessage` failure
0x80070032 (`ERROR_NOT_SUPPORTED`). The harness did not perform the policy
scope change, old-view write, fresh-open check, or writable-section attempt.
There is no section-denial or byte-privacy result from this run.

Cause assessment
----------------
The restored baseline policy, hash
29DC8A341BD7C549996596D2477A0CCCAF19602C362A2167FB4FDD5C66663731, has both
`removableDrives=true` and `networkPaths=true` and one destination path. In
`StageFence.c`, either candidate flag increments `CandidateVolumeScopesSkipped`;
when a candidate policy is being scanned, incomplete candidate coverage returns
`STATUS_NOT_SUPPORTED` before the policy swap (current source lines 1146–1155).
`Policy.c` returns that failed fence status before installing the new policy
(lines 409–416). This matches the observed Windows error and the user's
full-scope policy. The feature gate is therefore refusing a policy it cannot
yet prove across removable and network volumes.

Luna's independent source review found the SetPolicy command, protocol version,
and message size agree between service and driver; a protocol mismatch would
produce different statuses. The reviewer assessed the coverage gate as the most
likely immediate cause and found the D887 package mismatch unlikely. The exact
run22 unsigned-to-signed PE section identity and the guest service directory
match are recorded in `policy-transition-run22-shrink-artifact-provenance.txt`.
The exact `FailureLine` was not captured during the VM run, so the runtime
status-to-source attribution remains strongly supported rather than directly
reported by the driver status query.

The VM used the reviewed harness from commit `eca0bf7` (SHA-256
f09cd872b31a886249eb0ed970407cab33e74a63cac098d1f2492af7ca05b4b9). A later
comment-only correction updated its status note; the current hash
42B31CB502F013A3085C890BFE4DB0D0B86F7B36C4D03CE2473950A37D23424B was parsed
on the Windows builder with zero errors. No executable statement changed.

Restoration and scope
---------------------
The harness's `finally` restored the original installed driver and policy,
unloaded the feature filter, stopped the agent, removed mappings, fixture,
Inspector process/task/copy, and restored the original guest feature-input
file. The separate `Get-StagedBaseline.ps1` result is clean. The active disk is
the new preserved run22 external overlay recorded in
`policy-transition-run22-shrink-active-disk-after.txt`.

Do not turn off the removable/network policy flags to force this test forward;
that would narrow the approved scope. The next meaningful runtime attempt
requires scope-correct coverage for those policy flags. Any later section
diagnostic should also capture the driver's fence status and failure line if
policy acceptance is refused.
