# RV4 W-ledger ActivationGeneration sampling fix

Worktree: `/tmp/safeupload-rv4-w-ledger-20261005`  
Base: detached `6174dfda841132a0544dc1d96717207fc2392842`  
Scope: source-only correction to the reviewed ActivationGeneration race. No build, VM, Windows execution, or commit was performed.

`ActivationGeneration` was read under `StateLock`, but its writers ran under `RegistryLock`; the locks do not establish mutual exclusion. I changed the field to `volatile LONG`, added a compile-time field-alignment assertion, and changed every source access to an aligned 32-bit interlocked operation:

- The event snapshot uses `InterlockedCompareExchange` for its independent generation sample.
- The activation predicate uses `InterlockedCompareExchange`.
- Both generation publishers use `InterlockedExchange`, while retaining their existing `RegistryLock` protection for surrounding registry state.
- The activating-status page uses `InterlockedCompareExchange`.

This avoids acquiring `RegistryLock` from the elevated-IRQL callback snapshot and introduces no new lock ordering. It makes the generation value itself race-free; it does not make the generation, H/W/state, unknown reasons, or policy epoch one coherent tuple. The protocol comment and implementation notes now say that explicitly. W mutations remain serialized by `StateLock`; H and state/reason values are sampled context, and H is not made coherent with W by the lock flag.

Source audit: `rg -n "ActivationGeneration" driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c` finds only the declaration, the alignment assertion, two atomic reads in the trace/promotion paths, two atomic publishers, and the atomic status-page read. No plain load or store remains.
