## Generation fix

The previous draft sampled `ActivationGeneration` under `StateLock` while two writers assigned it under `RegistryLock`. The updated field is `volatile LONG` (`StageWriters.c:133`); all operational reads/writes now use aligned 32-bit interlocked operations: edge snapshot `:2517-2518`, promotion predicate `:4412`, the two publishers `:5561` and `:5635`, and status page `:5677`. A compile-time field-offset alignment assertion appears at `:165`. Source search shows no remaining ordinary access besides declaration/assertion. This avoids introducing `RegistryLock` into the elevated-IRQL snapshot and avoids a new lock-order edge.

The fix makes the generation sample race-free as an independent value. It does not serialize that value with H/W/state, UnknownReasons, or the global policy generation. The implementation comment (`StageWriters.c:2509-2512`) and wire comments (`Protocol.h:917,923`) now say so. H writers do not all hold `StateLock`; the `LOCKED` bit cannot support an H/W coherent tuple. W increment and retirement remain under `StateLock` (`StageWriters.c:2592-2603`, `2743-2754`). A fixture claiming H=0/W=1 must therefore use a stable externally controlled boundary and actual cleanup/lower receipts, not infer it from the flag alone.

## Rechecked observer lifetime and loss handling

The observation permit is acquired before the core W transition and retained until either the ticket or explicit loss is published (`StageWriters.c:2569-2571`, `2625-2684`). Thus pool exhaustion and missing completion-token paths count loss under the original permit. A ticket keeps the original core context and an extra entry reference in one of 128 aligned static slots; the wrapper is released only after core W retirement, snapshot, and record attempt (`StageWriters.c:89-105`, `2526-2541`, `2630-2675`, `2790-2806`). I found no source-only slot ABA, entry lifetime, or tag-alignment defect.

When W_END cannot record because the trace epoch is off, loss is incremented before the outstanding ticket count is decremented (`StageWriters.c:2488-2498`, `2790-2806`). DISABLE/CLEAR closes and drains recorder rundown, then rejects outstanding tickets or a changed lost count; CLEAR only resets on a drained, unchanged-loss window (`StageStream.c:294-329`). Begin holds rundown through the W admission/ticket decision, closing the reviewed pool-full race. Ring contention/overwrite increments loss (`StageStream.c:209-228`); such a trial is incomplete even if an end exists. Control/clear semantics still rely on arming/clearing before the trial and treating any lost count or unmatched/gapped pair as inconclusive.

Rename carries the same mutating context in the rename context (`StageStream.c:3391-3395`, `StageWriters.c:2825-2833`); the post path captures completion fields before rename completion retires the ticket (`StageStream.c:3564-3570`). Ordinary post and draining paths capture actual callback status/Information/flags before ending (`StageWriters.c:2702-2732`). A missing post is explicitly marked as pre-lower retirement with pending status (`:2796-2802`), not a lower completion receipt.
