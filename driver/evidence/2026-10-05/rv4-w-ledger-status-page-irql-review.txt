The local `STAGE_ACTIVATING_LOCKED_SNAPSHOT` is a bounded 40-byte kernel-stack object. Kernel stack is nonpaged; the caller's page is paged, but all page writes from this locked sample occur after the helper returns and lowers IRQL (:5702-5708). The registry entry allocation is explicitly nonpaged (`ExAllocatePool2(POOL_FLAG_NON_PAGED, ...)` at :582), so the fields read under StateLock remain resident. The helper adds no new lock, is called while the pre-existing shared RegistryLock remains held, and preserves RegistryLock-to-StateLock ordering. It reads/writes only bounded scalar/identity data under the spin lock and releases it before the caller reads T/S/name/PID/section samples.

The fix must retain that caveat and not change core locking merely for diagnostic output.
