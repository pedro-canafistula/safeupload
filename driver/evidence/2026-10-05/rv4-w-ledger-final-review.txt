## Blocker

`StageMutatingIoRefreshEntrySnapshot` reads `Entry->ActivationGeneration` as an ordinary load at `StageWriters.c:2513` while the shown writers assign it under `RegistryLock` at `StageWriters.c:5556` and `5629`. The sample's `StateLock` at `2509` does not serialize those assignments, and the code comment's claim that non-locked fields are atomically sampled does not hold for this field. A ticket can therefore carry a generation read racing a policy reconciliation; the trace cannot safely attribute that edge to the sampled generation. Use the same serialization for reads and writes, or make both sides use an aligned interlocked access and document that it is an independent instantaneous sample. Do not imply a coherent whole-entry tuple as part of the fix.

## Source findings that look sound, with limits

The previous pool-exhaustion loss window is closed in this snapshot. `BeginMutatingIo` takes the observer rundown permit before the core W increment (`StageWriters.c:2565-2571`) and holds it until the wrapper/loss decision is complete (`:2625-2684`). Ticket-pool exhaustion increments `LostEntries` under that permit (`:2671-2675`). On completion, an off-window W_END loss is counted while the ticket is still outstanding (`:2488-2498`, `:2790-2806`); disable/clear drains recorder permits and rejects while a ticket remains or the loss counter changed (`StageStream.c:294-329`). I found no new path where these two reported losses can be silently reset by CLEAR. Ring contention/overwrite also increments `LostEntries` (`StageStream.c:209-228`); such a run must be rejected as incomplete rather than interpreted as a paired ledger.

The ticket wrapper is bounded and resident: 128 aligned static slots, a distinct low-nibble tag, signature validation, a held core context, and an extra entry reference (`StageWriters.c:89-105`, `2526-2541`, `2630-2670`). End retires the core context before taking the post-retirement snapshot, then records and releases the extra reference (`:2790-2806`). That ordering preserves the entry object across retirement. The rename path transfers the same wrapper into the rename context (`:3391-3395` in `StageStream.c`; `StageWriters.c:2825-2833`) and its post path captures status before `CompleteRename` retires it (`StageStream.c:3564-3570`; `StageWriters.c:1912-1925`). Ticket exhaustion is visible, not a silent fallback. I found no source-only use-after-free, slot ABA, or lock-order deadlock in these paths.

The `LOCKED` snapshot flag is explicitly documented as “StateLock held during sample; not a whole-record seqlock” (`Protocol.h:308`, `906-923`). W increment/decrement use that lock (`StageWriters.c:2592-2603`, `2743-2754`), but H writers do not universally take it. Thus H is an atomic sample alongside W, not a coherent H/W tuple. Consumers may use a fixture-controlled stable boundary to claim H=0/W=1; a `LOCKED` bit alone cannot prove that overlap. The updated activating-status page adds W and captures H/W under the lock (`StageWriters.c:5656-5674`), with the same H caveat.

`CallbackData` is explicitly marked opaque in `Protocol.h:907`. The W ticket sequence proves that this upper-filter wrapper's own begin and end are paired. A raw callback-data address does not, on source evidence alone, prove identity with a lower-filter callback (or prevent address reuse); cross-filter correlation must also bind the exact operation, file identity, offset/length or operation parameters, and lower-stack receipts. W_END carries the actual callback `IoStatus.Status` and `Information` (`StageWriters.c:2718-2732`); draining and pre-lower-retirement cases are separately flagged. A status of `STATUS_PENDING` with the pre-lower-retirement bit is not evidence of a lower completion.

The write offset/length, FileId, volume serial, status, and ticket fields have explicit protocol size/offset assertions (`Protocol.h:1195-1235`). Inspector adds W event names and exact integer/ID formatting (`main.c:522-662`) and the literal formatter self-test is behind `SAFEUPLOAD_STAGING_PROTOTYPE` (`main.c:602-658`, `1675-1678`). StageWriters itself is enclosed by the same feature macro (`StageWriters.c:38`). This establishes source-level feature gating only. The retained builder report records Inspector normal and feature builds plus a literal mock formatter self-test; it is not a WDK minifilter compile, kernel hook-exclusion proof, or Windows runtime evidence.

## Qualification / decision

Do not use this snapshot to claim a qualified W01/W02 trial until the generation sampling race is corrected and the kernel is built and exercised on the required Windows 10 target. Even after that, source review cannot establish that cleanup/H=0 can overlap a queued real lower write, that the lower filter held/released the same IRP, that cancellation/draining happened as claimed, or that the final raw bytes and metadata match the real lower result. Those remain fixture/VM evidence requirements; unsupported or missing receipts must remain inconclusive.
