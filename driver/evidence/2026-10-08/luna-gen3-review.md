## Findings

- **P1 — By-ID Outside result can cross a pending-scope publication.** [StageWriters.c:4943] passes `NULL` as the classifier receipt, then [StageWriters.c:4946] copies the result to `InScope`. A concrete race: a mutating by-ID create acquires its epoch token and starts scanning; the scan’s final policy check at [StageWriters.c:4772] sees sequence N and completes Outside. Before `InScope` is consumed, `SetPolicy` publishes the current+pending union at [Policy.c:1080]; the publication advances the sequence under the cache lock at [Policy.c:531]. It then closes/drains the epoch at [Policy.c:1083]. The held token delays drain/finalization/reconciliation, but does not block that initial union publication. With no receipt check, [StageStream.c:2668] takes the Outside branch and passes the create to the filesystem for a file whose link is now in the pending protected scope. This is the prior P1-2 and remains. Mutating creates are epoch operations, and successful pass-through retains the token through lower completion ([StageStream.c:3197, 3645-57]); the TxF high-IRQL and null-FileObject branches conservatively refuse on possibly scoped volumes ([Txf.c:89-104]). Those facts do not close the pending-publication race.

- **P2 — A compact scan invalidated by a known directory move becomes permanently Unknown.** [StageWriters.c:1941] now bumps compact entries’ `RenameVersion` when a directory rename completes and begins their newer probe. Interleaving: a compact entry’s reclaim scan captures versions; an unrelated successful directory rename completes on the same instance (compact entries have no name for path filtering), so [StageWriters.c:1938-42] advances the probe serial and rename version; the scan’s stable check at [StageWriters.c:4788-4809] returns `STATUS_FILE_INVALID`. The alias-probe error path at [StageWriters.c:5605-07] resolves that as failed classification; [StageWriters.c:5296-5322] marks sticky `Unknown(IDENTITY)` and clears `AliasProbePending`. The queued rescan then skips the Unknown entry. One ordinary successful directory move during a scan can therefore withhold coverage permanently even after the filesystem becomes quiet. A completed scan whose *receipt* is stale does correctly retain `AliasProbePending`; the worker detects it at [StageWriters.c:6064-68] and queues another pass. The failure above is the earlier publish-time invalidation, before that receipt path.
