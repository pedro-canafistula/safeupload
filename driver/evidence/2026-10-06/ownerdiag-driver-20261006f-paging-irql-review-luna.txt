Placement findings
------------------
The PE/PDB readout identifies PAGE as section 5, characteristics 0x60000020
(code, execute, read; not discardable). PDB procedure records place the owner
ECP functions in that section, including SafeUploadOwnerAuthorizeFspCreate,
SafeUploadOwnerRegisterFspVolume, SafeUploadOwnerClassifyVolume,
SafeUploadOwnerIssueCreateGrant, SafeUploadOwnerAttachCreateEcp,
SafeUploadOwnerDisconnect, and SafeUploadOwnerTeardownInstance. StageOwnerEcp.c
sets code_seg("PAGE") before its implementation and resets it at EOF. Its
pageable routines contain PAGED_CODE checks. The instance-admission and
coverage status routines are explicitly allocated to PAGE and check
PAGED_CODE. They are status/control paths, not the high-IRQL write-completion
path.

The paging-write admission wrapper SafeUploadStageWritersBeginPagingIo and
SafeUploadStageDispatch are in .text. The wrapper declares APC_LEVEL maximum;
the shared admission helper checks KeGetCurrentIrql() <= APC_LEVEL before
touching stream contexts. EndMutatingIo/completion helpers and the section/SOP
spin-lock updates remain in .text and are annotated or implemented for up to
DISPATCH_LEVEL. The reviewed path does not call a PAGE helper from its
DISPATCH_LEVEL completion callback.

Lock-balance findings
---------------------
The OwnerFspLock wrappers disable normal kernel APC delivery with
KeEnterCriticalRegion before ExAcquirePushLockShared/Exclusive and pair every
release with ExReleasePushLock followed by KeLeaveCriticalRegion. I followed
all conditional exits in BuildFspTargetName, BindRequestToGeneration,
LookupGeneration, ReleaseEpoch, GetScope, IssueCreateGrant,
AuthorizeFspCreate, Disconnect, and TeardownInstance; each acquired scope has
a release on every return/goto path. Referenced process, instance, grant, and
route objects are released after dropping OwnerFspLock on the reviewed paths.
The paging W-count path uses the separate StateLock/SectionLock spin-lock
wrappers and releases them before queuing rechecks or marking uncertain state.
No unmatched critical-region or push-lock release was found in this slice.

Review boundary / hardening item
-------------------------------
StageOwnerEnsureLockInitialized uses an interlocked 0 -> -1 -> 1 state and
YieldProcessor while another caller is in the short nonblocking initialization
sequence. SafeUploadOwnerAuthorizeFspCreate can reach it from the WinFsp
IRP_MJ_CREATE pre-operation path before owner registration. This review did
not establish a deadlock: the winning initialization has no blocking calls,
and the function is explicitly pageable/APC-safe. Eager initialization at
DriverEntry before FltStartFiltering would nevertheless remove the first-use
spin and avoid any possible reentrant APC waiting on an interrupted
initializer. Treat this as hardening, not as an observed runtime failure.
