Continue only if every pre-existing allow principal is explicitly approved for this disposable builder and the effective result grants vika only the exact constructed Read|Synchronize rule beyond the approved baseline. If any extra/unexpected principal or ACL semantic/order change appears, preserve the failed branch and restore the verified pre-fallback baseline; do not import trust, change source pins, build, or sign.
