# Private ownership before mapped sections: integrated M1/M2 plan

Design plan, 2026-10-06. This records the smallest coherent path against the
current implementation. It does not change product source or either repro,
and makes no build, runtime, or qualification claim.

## Boundary and decision

The owner-approved setup is: the privacy owner runs before application writable
opens; an initial restart is allowed; policy inspection and publication may be
activated afterward. The owner allocator therefore must be available before
the fixture open, but it must not treat allocation as policy approval or publish
an owner-only version. If “service activation afterward” meant that no service
process or durable allocator may run before the first open, the current
StageStream path cannot meet it: every new StageCreate synchronously requests a
flushed service Allocated journal entry and stage basename, and StageSeal also
uses that service channel. A kernel-owned durable allocator/journal would be a
different, substantially larger architecture. This plan uses a narrowly scoped
allocation-only service role before the open, with policy/inspection/publication
still delayed.

The existing owned-stream architecture is the integration point. It leaves the
application handle on the destination stack, gives the application a distinct
SafeUpload upper stream with a private section/cache object, and routes paging
and flush I/O to a real private NTFS stage file. It does not borrow the public
destination's NTFS FCB or SOP. This matches the original same-stack decision in
`STAGED-WRITES.md`; the rejected cross-volume reparse experiment changed visible
volume identity and left the user able to read the private stage after filter
unload. The custom upper SOP is SafeUpload's private stream state, not the
public destination SOP. Keep this distinction explicit and rerun its exact
mapping/Verifier qualification on the new source.

## Existing hooks and required changes

1. **Bounded owner scope before filtering.** `BootPolicy.c` currently loads the
   authenticated boot/current/pending destination scopes; it has no separate
   owner-candidate scope. Add a protected, versioned `OwnerScopes` record to
   the same LocalSystem/TrustedInstaller policy boundary. It is a finite list
   of canonical local fixed-NTFS prefixes that is a superset of every policy
   scope the MVP may activate, including the M1/M2 fixtures. Load and validate
   it before `FltStartFiltering`; do not enumerate the filesystem. Missing,
   malformed, incomplete, or unsupported owner scope means `OwnerReady=false`
   and candidate writes fail closed. Policy expansion outside this set is
   refused and cannot report Ready until owner scope is expanded at reboot.

2. **Route before any public writable object or section.** In feature builds,
   `Filter.c` sends creates through `StageDispatchCore`, which calls
   `StageAdmit` before `SafeUploadPreCreate` and the public-writer reservation.
   Add an owner-candidate check there, before the current-policy pass-through.
   A mutating create under an owner prefix must enter the existing `StageCreate`
   path and receive a distinct private `STAGE_STREAM`/SOP before the create
   completes; it must never fall through to NTFS. Read-only opens outside the
   active policy remain on the approved public generation. Preserve unresolved
   name, alias, ADS, by-ID, reparse, TxF, directory, and unsupported-option
   refusal behavior; do not broaden supported filesystems or adapters.

3. **Durable owner allocation, separate from policy.** `StageCreate` calls
   `SafeUploadStageAllocate` before opening its private noncached backing and
   before returning the upper file object. Extend that request with an explicit
   owner-only mode and make the service validate it against the same protected
   `OwnerScopes` record and supported-volume rules. The service must flush the
   exact private-file ACL and `Allocated` journal entry before returning a
   basename. Owner-only allocation must not set active policy, authorize a
   publication permit, scan, inspect, or publish. Require the connected service
   identity for this operation; a generic SYSTEM connection alone is not the
   owner allocator.

4. **Permit private writes before policy activation.** The private stream's
   writable section gate is in `StagePreOperation` for
   `IRP_MJ_ACQUIRE_FOR_SECTION_SYNCHRONIZATION`; today it also requires an
   authenticated policy client. Add a narrowly tagged owner-only state bound
   to the verified owner scope, trusted newly mounted NTFS instance, durable
   allocation, and live stream generation. Such a stream may create writable
   sections and may write/flush only through its private StageStream cache and
   backing. Every non-owned/public stream retains the existing trust and active
   policy gates. If ownership cannot be proved, refuse the create before the
   destination filesystem receives a writable object.

5. **Hold early generations out of publication.** The worker in `StageStream.c`
   drains only after no upper file objects and `MmCanFileBeTruncated` confirms
   the private SOP is quiescent, purges its cache, waits for paging rundown,
   flushes the private backing, then asks the service to seal. Today a successful
   seal enters `Sealed`, and `PublishSealedLoopAsync` starts inspection and
   publication for every such entry. Add an append-only journal state or
   durable owner-only flag such as `OwnerPendingPolicy`: owner-only sealing
   records an immutable private version but is not eligible for inspection or
   publication. After the active policy is durably accepted, the service may
   promote only owner-pending entries whose exact destination and generation
   are now in policy to normal `Sealed`; the existing inspection, approval,
   permit, and publication path then applies. Nonmatching entries remain private
   and retained. Recovery must preserve the nonpublishable state across crashes.

6. **Readiness and lifetime.** Report owner readiness separately from policy
   activation. Owner readiness requires the bounded scope record, newly mounted
   volume canary, allocator/journal availability, and complete candidate-scope
   coverage. It does not mean the policy is active or a version is approved.
   The existing per-process `StageView` means its writer sees its private
   generation; another process sees the current public approved generation.
   WinFsp's pinned cleanup/close study is lifecycle guidance only: retain
   generation state beyond Cleanup and Close, and reclaim only after the exact
   section/cache and paging work are gone. Do not use a cleanup notification as
   proof that a retained mapping has ended. A late/manual attach with preexisting
   public writable mappings stays NotReady until reboot; the owner-approved
   initial restart is the boundary for a clean start.

## Repro and proof changes

- **M1:** start the boot owner before creating/opening the fixture writable;
  let the allocation-only owner create a private upper stream; create the
  writable section and close the source handle; delay active policy, inspection,
  and publication. Preserve successful mapped write, view flush, release,
  successful `FlushFileBuffers`, independent buffered and uncached reads,
  unchanged full raw bytes, and stable base allocation. The observer readers
  must run in a separate process: today's M1 helper reopens in the same
  PowerShell process and would see its own per-process private `StageView`.
  This becomes a pre-policy activation test, not a pre-owner/pre-attachment
  test.
- **M2:** start the owner before any fixture open. Open and create the retained
  writable section while the fixture is outside the active policy but inside
  the immutable owner candidate scope. Expand policy while retaining its
  section handle, create the first view afterward, and require successful write,
  view flush, and file-buffer flush. Expansion must keep the same private
  generation/SOP; it must not re-open or rebind the section. Run buffered,
  uncached, and raw observers from separate observer processes while the
  writer mapping is held. `Ready`/publication stays pending until owner drain,
  then promotion and inspection are tied to the exact sealed generation.
- **Security and regression:** exercise owner-scope boundaries, all existing
  path/identity refusal cases, aliases, rename/link, ADS, file-ID opens,
  concurrent owner processes, service loss/restart, policy expansion, stage
  capacity/allocation errors, and crash recovery. A failed allocation, unknown
  identity, incomplete scope, or lost journal record is a refusal/NotReady
  result, never a privacy pass. Verify private-stage ACLs and direct-name
  isolation after driver/service restart. Keep taint and unsupported destinations
  guarded; regular builds continue compiling staging out.

## Acceptance boundary

This change cannot close the two mapping DOD items by source inspection alone.
The exact M1/M2 successful-operation predicates and independent observers remain
mandatory. The larger MVP DOD remains unchanged: four normal/feature Debug/Release
builds with zero warnings/errors, managed tests after service/journal changes,
both corrected repros, runtime and boot Verifier, independent raw observers,
USB/UNC/real-sync qualification, recovery/restoration, and the remaining
functional, crash, stress, and latency gates. The qualified Ready4 source and
artifacts become historical after any source change; all builds and guest
qualification must pin the new exact tree. No taint removal or unsupported
capability enablement follows from this integration plan.
