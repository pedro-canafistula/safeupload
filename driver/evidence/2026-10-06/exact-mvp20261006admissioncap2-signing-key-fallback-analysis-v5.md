## V4 independent review and V5 ACL correction

The frozen V4 creator had a concrete post-write failure: the memory-only Windows PowerShell 5.1 model constructed `FileSystemAccessRule(SID, Read, Allow)` as mask `1179785` (`Read, Synchronize`), while V4 required the final ACE to equal the raw `Read` enum mask `131209`. A normal newly added ACE would therefore fail its guard after `Set-Acl`. The model readout is `signing-filesystem-read-ace-model.stdout.json`; it confirms that the probe changed no state and opened no key or file.

V4 also rejected broad `Everyone`, `Authenticated Users`, or `Users` allow ACEs only after attempting its ACL update. That could leave a changed ACL despite the stop. V5 rejects those ACEs, plus missing SYSTEM or Administrators allow ACEs, before calling `Set-Acl`. It creates the same in-memory `FileSystemAccessRule` used for the grant, derives the exact expected mask and ACE row from that object, and requires the constructed mask to equal `Read | Synchronize`. It rejects an existing target-SID FullControl or other non-exact ACE and never broadens it. After a possible write, V5 requires the exact original ACE multiset plus exactly one constructed rule, no removals, and unchanged owner, group, and ACL protection state. Immediately before `Set-Acl`, it re-reads and compares SDDL, owner, group, protection state, and ACE rows to avoid applying a stale ACL snapshot.

The memory-only `exact-mvp20261006admissioncap2-signing-guard-fixture-v5.ps1` exercises the actual shared guard helper: it accepts the exact one-ACE delta and typed code-signing EKU, and rejects FullControl, extra rights, unexpected or removed ACEs, broad allow ACEs, owner/group/protection drift, duplicate EKU extensions/purposes, and malformed EKU bytes. The fixture is scoped to Windows PowerShell 5.1, writes no files, and does not inspect stores, use ACL cmdlets, open keys, import trust, or contact a VM. It has not been run in this authoring step.
