Material blocker

The fresh `check_preflight()` called by the shutdown phase obtains the live
QMP image chain, but requires only that it begins with retry2 then Recovery3
and that retry1 is absent. It does not compare the entire fresh chain with
the exact reviewed captured chain from the pinned preflight. The later
`verify-host` phase compares the post-start QMP chain to that older captured
chain, so a change in a deeper backing ancestor can pass the fresh
pre-shutdown guard and be discovered only after the new child has booted.
Before mutating phases are approved, require full equality between the fresh
chain and the expected pinned full chain during `check_preflight()` before
requesting shutdown. The actual target chain was reported stable; this is a
missing fail-closed comparison, not evidence of an observed chain change.

Parent-stat boundary assessment

An initial concern that the helper did not compare the running parent
byte-size and modification time to the earlier live preflight capture is
withdrawn. That earlier capture explicitly allows normal guest writes while
the VM is running and graceful shutdown is pending. The correct freeze point
is the first full parent stat after shut-off; V2 records it before child
creation and checks the full stat remains unchanged through child creation
and cold boot. The independent fresh V13 semantic baseline is the guest
health evidence before shutdown. Do not require equality to the earlier
running-image size or mtime.
