If Y has H=2, the tested actor can release one holder, another holder can release later, and that later CAS plus the final H=0 query still falls inside the tested actor-release/query window. Require H=1 and the sole opener PID to be the tested actor at the immediate pre-release checkpoint, or capture proof that the supplied release is the last-holder release. The supplied run's H=1 evidence satisfies this condition.
- **P1 — bind the CAS to the held incarnation.** The adapter accepts predicate bit `0x20` (`INCARNATION_REPLACED`) and joins the held receipt, CAS, and final query by file ID and volume serial. R02's held predicate does not check or bind an instance/SOP identity. A same-ID stream replacement can therefore let a CAS for a replacement incarnation qualify while the original held incarnation is still live. For R02, reject bit `0x20` unless the held-to-CAS incarnation transition is independently proven; alternatively bind a stable incarnation token across the held receipt, CAS, and final query. This run has flags 15, so rejecting `0x20` would not reject this evidence.

Keep that assertion and gate state unchanged, and do not describe the repair as proving the whole-interval Ready invariant.
