HEAD `bd4d02fb` differs from parent `7c316ebb` only in `StageStream.c`: it splits cache flushing from the full backing flush and uses the cache-only part during growth.

**Ordering and confidentiality:** The new `StageFlushCache` still calls `CcFlushCache` and checks its status. `StageZeroGrowth` calls it before reading a partial sector and writing the zeroed extension; `StageResize` updates the backing EOF and upper file size/VDL only after those writes complete. The aligned noncached writes, partial-tail preservation, and VDL sequence are unchanged ([flush split](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:1070>), [growth path](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:1504>), [resize commit](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:1548>)). The backing is opened noncached and rejected if it already has a cache or section map ([backing open](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:1142>)).

The definite durability change is narrower: growth no longer synchronizes the lower storage cache for earlier dirty data as a side effect. `CcFlushCache` can wait for cached data to be flushed; `FltFlushBuffers` synchronously issues a flush and synchronizes the underlying storage cache ([Microsoft: CcFlushCache](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/ntifs/nf-ntifs-ccflushcache), [Microsoft: FltFlushBuffers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/fltkernel/nf-fltkernel-fltflushbuffers)). That stable-media guarantee remains on explicit flush, write-through, cleanup, and seal/drain paths ([write-through and flush dispatch](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:1628>), [cleanup](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:2094>), [drain](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:2233>)). I find no new live-read exposure from this change. It does not establish a power-loss guarantee for the zeroed extension: the old device flush occurred before the zero writes, and the project’s durability notes already leave sudden-power-loss persistence unqualified ([design note](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/STAGED-WRITES.md:2765>)).
