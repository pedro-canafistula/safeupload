# REJECT for controlled runtime-Verifier use of `847a21da`

The core freshness correction is sound, and a `Ready` frame **cannot erase an already latched `Ready`**. But a queued frame can still be discarded before it reaches the latch, and several status assertions still mishandle generation or protection state.

## Blocking findings

1. **Closing a stream can drop an unread `Ready`.** The latch and history are updated only after a frame is parsed in [`Get-ActivationProductStatus`](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:876>). But [`Close-ActivationNotificationCapture`](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:840>) disposes the reader and pipe, then waits for the pending `ReadTask` without retrieving or recording its result. Every fresh checkpoint calls this close before opening a new connection ([lines 899–901](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:899>)). A completed read containing `Ready` can therefore be discarded; a later connection can snapshot `Pending`, leaving the final latch empty. The fixture exercises queued `Ready` then `Pending` while draining one connection, but not while replacing a connection with a pending or completed read ([fixture](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/evidence/2026-10-07/sol-notification-stream-corrected-fixture.ps1:16>)). Drain pending results through the same latch before close, or make the affected observation inconclusive when a read is discarded; add a control for that replacement case.

2. **Generation and protection checks are still inconsistent.** The corrected `knownReady` and `readyWhileHeld` predicates now require the accepted generation and active protection. However, the separate `ServiceNeverReadyAtSampleAfterMutation` failure marks **any** `Ready` sample as FAIL ([lines 2877–2880](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:2877>)), so a `Ready` from another generation or with protection inactive can falsely fail the trial. After promotion, `knownDegraded` also fails on `Degraded` without checking the accepted generation ([lines 2928–2931](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:2928>)). Conversely, `ServiceStillReadyDuringOwnedWrite` can PASS for the accepted generation without requiring `protectionActive` ([lines 2986–2988](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:2986>)). Apply the relevant generation and active-protection guards consistently; treat unmatched observations as inconclusive.

## Corrections that hold

Fresh checkpoint calls close the old capture and select only the first status from a new connection. The service sends its `GetRecordedStatus()` snapshot before queued notifications on that connection ([`NotificationPipeServer.cs`](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Notifications/NotificationPipeServer.cs:171>), [`NotificationHub.cs`](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Notifications/NotificationHub.cs:168>)). Retained stream state now returns INCONCLUSIVE with its original receipt, and transition waits accept only newly read statuses. The local receipt QPC still says when the harness read a frame; it does not provide a server publication time or loss-free interval proof.

For frames that are actually parsed, the latch requires the exact candidate generation, active protection, `Ready`, and a live holder ([lines 881–882](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:881>)). The finalizer preserves that failure even after a later `Pending` or an aborted observation ([lines 3017–3018](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:3017>)).

The Inspector deadline checks now cover before connection attempts, after open, and after SYSTEM authentication ([`StagedProofProxy.h`](</home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Inspector/StagedProofProxy.h:105>)). Retries remain limited to connection establishment; there is no resend or driver-port fallback. A synchronous service-side `FilterSendMessage` still cannot be cancelled by the Inspector timeout.
