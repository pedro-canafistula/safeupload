A send lasting over five seconds therefore finishes with an already-canceled response token; the pending-send test encodes that behavior ([AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Diagnostics/AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs:288), [StagedProofProxyTests.cs](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Tests/StagedProofProxyTests.cs:190)). The send lease remains safe, but clarify the contract: start the timer at the first response write for a fresh five-second write window, or describe it as an absolute response cutoff measured from request handling.
