# Review: `82e2e951` — Stamp activation generation at begin

**Verdict: REJECT**

**Finding: P1 — the new stamp can still authorize a promotion after the policy generation changes.**

## Finding

[`StageWriters.c:5172`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5172) stamps `ActivationGeneration` from the current generation when `BeginAliasProbe` runs. That makes a fresh runtime activation eligible to promote, fixing the observed generation-0 deferral. The generation is intended to bind classification to a policy version: link-name classification records its start generation at [`StageWriters.c:4492`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:4492) and rejects a scan if it changes before publication at [`StageWriters.c:4736`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:4736). That protects a policy change during the scan, but not a commit after a stable old-policy result and before its CAS. The final generation predicate is not atomic with the policy update: `StageRegistryReclaimOne` samples `currentGeneration` at [`StageWriters.c:5679`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5679), and `StageRegistryTryPromoteEntry` compares the entry against that passed sample at [`StageWriters.c:5103`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5103). It does not reread the live generation at the state CAS ([`StageWriters.c:5120`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5120)).

Concrete interleaving (G is the old generation; G+1 is the committed policy):

1. A pending-policy apply has already visited an entry and assigned G+1 at [`StageWriters.c:6634`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:6634). Before finalization, a runtime rename completion begins another probe; the new line 5172 overwrites that future stamp with G.
2. The reclaim worker classifies the old current scope under G, records `ScopeNameClassification=SCOPED`, completes its writer/S/cache checks, then samples `currentGeneration=G` at line 5679.
3. Finalization publishes the pending policy at [`Policy.c:1175`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1175), then increments the generation at [`Policy.c:1178`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1178). It can do this while the worker holds `RegistryLock`; the subsequent reconcile call at [`Policy.c:1183`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1183) then waits for that lock.
4. The worker reaches the CAS using its saved G. Line 5103 passes even though the live generation is now G+1, and line 5120 can change Activating to Protected on the old classification. The policy-generation increment does not take `RegistryLock` or the entry `StateLock`. The rename-loss lock held around the promotion checks rename-loss state, not policy generation. The `PolicyGeneration` captured by `SafeUploadPolicyReadLiveSnapshot` at line 5674 is explicitly a sample; it is not compared with `CurrentGeneration` or revalidated at the CAS.

The policy epoch drain does not close this window: the registry reclaim pass is a queued generic work item (`StageRegistryReclaimWorker`, [`StageWriters.c:5870`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5870)) and does not acquire an admission-epoch token. Its identity open requests only `FILE_READ_ATTRIBUTES | SYNCHRONIZE` with `FILE_OPEN` ([`StageWriters.c:4196`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:4196)); read-only opens are outside the mutating operations in `StageEpochOperation` ([`StageStream.c:3194`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageStream.c:3194)). Reconcile runs after the increment and may later clear or redo the entry, but that does not prevent this stale promotion. For example, if G scoped a name and G+1 removes it, the stale worker can briefly publish Protected using G's classification after G+1 is committed. This is fail-closed in that example, but it violates the stated no-old-policy-promotion invariant. The new stamp turns a fresh runtime activation that previously failed the generation predicate into one that can take this path.

## Claim and path audit

The evidence supports the narrow diagnosis for fresh runtime entries at policy generation 1:

- C03's `final-not-ready--activating-status.out` has the `cached.txt` row at generation 0, policy generation 1, `Activating`, and `PromoteDeferred` status `0xE0000200`; its other listed quiescence values are clear (`H/C/T/W=0`, `S=NO`, no unknown reasons).
- C01's corresponding status artifact has 18 `latency-000.txt` through `latency-017.txt` rows, all `Activating`, generation 0, while policy generation is 1.

Before this commit, the four rename-completion paths at [`StageWriters.c:1925`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:1925), [`StageWriters.c:1946`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:1946), [`StageWriters.c:2205`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:2205), and [`StageWriters.c:2244`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:2244) called `StageRegistryBeginAliasProbe` without stamping. A fresh row therefore stayed at zero; at generation 1 it could not pass the only activation-generation predicate in `StageRegistryTryPromoteEntry` (line 5103). There is no other nonzero writer I found: the writers are now BeginAliasProbe (5172), pending scope apply (6634, G+1), and current-scope reconcile (6710, current G). New entry insertion records `FirstSeenGeneration`, not `ActivationGeneration` ([`StageWriters.c:1192`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:1192)).

The word “never” in the commit rationale is too broad. A zero-initialized activation generation can equal the live generation when that generation is still zero. Also, promotion does not clear `ActivationGeneration`; an entry re-probed after a prior activation can retain the current generation and be promotable without a new stamp, provided it is reclassified and the other predicates pass. These do not explain the C01/C03 generation-0 rows at policy generation 1, but they are counterexamples to the universal premise.

The other relevant paths are consistent with that audit: transaction association changes can re-arm `AliasProbePending` at [`StageWriters.c:842`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:842), and a transaction/version mismatch can do so at [`StageWriters.c:5326`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5326); neither writes `ActivationGeneration`. `StageRegistryPrepareActivation` can set Activating at line 5141, and alias resolution / versioned classification can set it at lines 5210, 5221, and 5330. Those paths do not add another generation stamp. The Protected transition is the CAS in `StageRegistryTryPromoteStateNoInline` at line 4920.

## Policy-update race matrix

In the staging path, `SafeUploadSetPolicy` publishes current-plus-pending scope state, drains the old admission epoch, and runs `ApplyPendingScope` ([`Policy.c:1098`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1098)). That pass assigns G+1 to entries it visits. The pending policy is finalized later: finalization drains again, publishes it as current, increments the policy generation, then reconciles ([`Policy.c:1178`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1178)).

| Rename completion timing | Entry generation at begin / CAS | Stale-policy result |
|---|---|---|
| Before `ApplyPendingScope` visits the entry | Begin writes G. If apply gets the entry first, it resets the probe and overwrites with G+1. If the reclaim worker gets to the final CAS first, it can still carry G while the live generation is G. | After apply, G+1 blocks a normal CAS until finalization. A CAS that beats apply is before the generation commit; if its sampled G is carried across the later commit, the P1 race above applies. |
| Between the apply pass and the generation increment | Begin writes G and can regress the apply pass's G+1 stamp. At a CAS before increment, G matches the still-current generation. If the worker sampled G before the increment and CASes after it, the stale promotion is possible. | **Yes**, for a worker that crosses the increment with a saved G. This is the clearest case introduced by the new write. |
| After increment, before reconcile visits the entry | Begin writes G+1 and clears `ScopeNameClassification` to unresolved. The subsequent scan starts under G+1; old partial scan results are keyed by policy generation and are discarded when the generation changed. | No stale promotion from this new probe; the entry cannot pass until it gets a new scoped classification. An already-running worker can still hit the general stale-sample race if it had captured G before the increment and the entry was not reset by this Begin. |
| Concurrent with `ReconcileCurrentScope` | Rename completion and reconcile both hold `RegistryLock`, so they serialize for a given entry. Whichever begins after the increment stamps G+1; reconcile also explicitly overwrites with G+1. | The Begin/Reconcile field update itself is safe. A reclaim worker holding `RegistryLock` first can delay reconcile while the generation increments independently, so the stale-sample promotion above remains possible. |

## Protected/Unscoped entries and IRQL

Stamping inside `BeginAliasProbe` does not itself promote a Protected entry. For `T=0`, Begin sets `AliasProbePending` and makes scope classification unresolved but leaves the state Protected; the promotion predicate requires state Activating at [`StageWriters.c:5093`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/StageWriters.c:5093). A later successful union-scope resolution changes it to Activating. With `T!=0`, Begin also sets Activating and keeps the entry enforced. Thus a Protected entry with a pending probe but still Protected cannot be promoted or otherwise state-changed by the generation write alone.

The read is IRQL-safe: `SafeUploadCurrentPolicyGeneration` is a nonpaged, lock-free `InterlockedCompareExchange` accessor at [`Policy.c:1218`](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-replaced/driver/SafeUpload.Minifilter/Policy.c:1218), and it is not in the `alloc_text(PAGE, ...)` list. Calling it while `StateLock` raises to DISPATCH_LEVEL adds no lock-order edge. That only establishes IRQL safety; the interlocked read does not serialize the stamp with a later generation increment.
