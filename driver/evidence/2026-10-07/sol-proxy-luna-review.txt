- **Port lifetime:** The relay sends through the service’s existing `FilterPort` and its send lease. Binding drain prevents a send from outliving the port; I found no disconnect/reconnect path. The A-path helper routes Inspector calls through the relay while the service is marked running, and uses direct Inspector calls before startup and after stop. See [AdmissionEvidenceCapture.cs](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Diagnostics/AdmissionEvidenceCapture.cs:110) and [Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/scripts/Test-StagedInvariantSuite.ps1:773).

- **Timeout limitation:** The Inspector’s 10-second deadline bounds its overlapped named-pipe I/O. It does **not** cancel a synchronous native `FilterSendMessage` already running in the service: the service’s cancellation token is applied to response writes after that call. If the send stalls, the Inspector can time out while the service listener remains occupied; unbind drains the send before cancelling the listener. This does not violate the stated *I/O* deadline, but it must not be represented as a 10-second bound on the whole request. Before relying on timeout behavior, exercise slow-send, client-disconnect, and shutdown cases; a true whole-request cancellation guarantee needs a safe design that preserves port lifetime. See [StagedProofProxy.h](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/driver/SafeUpload.Inspector/StagedProofProxy.h:30), [AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Diagnostics/AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs:288), and [AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs](/home/victor/Work/safeupload-wt-sol/agente/SafeUpload.Agent.Service/Diagnostics/AdmissionEvidenceEndpoint.cs:105).
